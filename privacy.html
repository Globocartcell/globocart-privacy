GloboCart Privacy Policy

Effective date: 15 October 2026 · Applies to the GloboCart Android app (com.globocart.app), the GloboCart web app (PWA) and globocart.com

This policy explains what personal data GloboCart (“we”, “us”) collects when you use the GloboCart marketplace, why we collect it, who we share it with, and the choices and rights you have. Contact: devops@globocart.com.

1. Data we collect

Data you give us

  • Account: email address, password (stored only as a one-way argon2 hash), language and time zone. If you choose Google or Apple sign-in, we receive your email and account identifier from that provider and keep a link to that sign-in account. If you enable them, we store multi-factor (TOTP) settings and passkey (WebAuthn) public credentials.
  • Addresses and contact: names, shipping and billing addresses, phone number and (optionally) a tax ID on an address. The phone number and tax ID on buyer addresses are encrypted at rest.
  • Orders and activity: orders, cart, wishlist, saved searches, reviews, buyer–seller messages, disputes and dispute evidence (text and links you paste), and referral codes.
  • Seller information (sellers only): legal or business name, country, registration number, tax ID, identity/business verification (KYC/KYB) status, storefront details, and listings with photos. Seller registration numbers and tax IDs are currently stored without field-level encryption (they are protected by our general database and access controls). Payout account details are held by our payment processor and are shown to us only in masked form.
  • Location (optional): your home country. In the web app only, if you tap “save location” in Account and allow it in your browser, your precise position is stored. The Android app does not request the location permission, so in the app only your country is stored. Location is used for delivery estimates and local results and is never collected in the background.
  • Photos you upload (sellers only): listing photos that sellers choose with the system file picker. Buyers do not upload photos; reviews have no images, and dispute or return evidence is provided as text or links.
  • Crypto wallet (optional, demo feature): if you use “Connect wallet”, we store the wallet address and chain you enter and the time it was linked. This is included in your data export.

Payment data

Card payments (Visa and Mastercard) are processed by our payment processor, Aegis, or by Stripe where the operator enables it. Your full card number and CVV are entered on the processor’s payment page and handled by the processor. GloboCart does not receive or store them. To create a payment we send the processor the order ID, amount, currency and your email address. We keep the payment provider, status, amount, currency and the processor’s session and payment IDs, needed for orders, refunds, escrow and disputes.

Data collected automatically

  • Server logs: our servers log each request, including your IP address in full, the requested URL, your browser/device user agent and other request headers. Session cookies and authorization headers are redacted from these logs.
  • Security and fraud prevention: in our audit and risk records we store a one-way hash of your IP address, sign-in events, session information and order risk signals used by our fraud engine and sanctions screening.
  • Audit trail: a tamper-evident audit log of security-relevant actions.
  • Usage within the service: recently viewed products, search queries and, for sellers, aggregate listing analytics. We do not use third-party advertising or analytics SDKs, and we do not sell your data or use it for cross-app tracking.
  • Push notifications (web app, optional): if you allow notifications in your browser, a push subscription is stored so we can send order, message and dispute updates. The Android app does not currently send push notifications.
  • Cookies and local storage: gc_session (sign-in) and gc_csrf (security); gc_ref (the referral code you arrived with, kept 30 days) and gc_ref_vid (a random visitor ID used to attribute referrals, kept 90 days); and local storage for preferences such as language, currency and cart. There are no advertising cookies.

2. How we use data

  • To run your account and the marketplace: listings, checkout, payments, escrow (funds are held until delivery is confirmed), shipping, refunds and disputes.
  • For security, fraud and abuse prevention, sanctions screening, and content moderation.
  • For customer support and service messages (email, and push in the web app).
  • To attribute referrals.
  • Optional AI features, when enabled: translation of listings, reviews and messages, and the AI shopping assistant. The text you submit is sent to our AI provider (Anthropic) to produce the result. When you use the shopping assistant while signed in, your 5 most recent orders (order ID, status, total, currency, order and delivery dates, and item titles and quantities) are also sent as context, together with matching catalog listings. This data is used only to answer your request.
  • To meet legal duties such as tax, invoicing, accounting and anti-money-laundering.

Our legal bases (GDPR/UK GDPR) are: performance of the contract, legitimate interests (security, fraud prevention, referral attribution and service improvement), legal obligation, and your consent for optional location, push notifications and AI features. You can withdraw consent at any time.

3. Sharing

We share only what each party needs:

  • Sellers and buyers: when you order, the seller receives your name, shipping address and order details. Buyers see a seller’s public storefront.
  • Payment processors: Aegis, and Stripe where enabled, for payments, refunds and payouts.
  • Shipping carriers: DHL, FedEx, UPS or a regional courier receive the recipient name, address, phone and parcel details for rates and labels.
  • Service providers: hosting, database, file storage (Amazon S3), email delivery, browser push services, and our AI provider (Anthropic) for translation and the shopping assistant. All of them act on our instructions.
  • Sign-in providers: Google and Apple, if you use them.
  • Authorities: where required by law, or to protect users and prevent fraud.

We do not sell personal data, and we do not share it for targeted advertising.

4. Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS). Passwords are hashed with argon2, and the phone number and tax ID on buyer addresses are encrypted at rest. We offer optional MFA and passkeys and use role-based staff access, rate limiting and a tamper-evident audit log. Data exports exclude credentials and bank details. No system is perfectly secure, and we do not claim any security certification we do not hold.

5. Retention

  • Account data is kept while your account is open. What happens on deletion is listed on the delete-account page.
  • Order, invoice, payment and KYC records are kept for as long as tax and anti-money-laundering law requires, typically 6 to 10 years, in pseudonymised form after an account is deleted.
  • Server, security and audit logs are kept for as long as needed for security and legal purposes.
  • Sessions expire. A push subscription is removed when you turn notifications off in the web app.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live (EU/EEA GDPR, UK GDPR, California CCPA/CPRA, Pakistan’s Prevention of Electronic Crimes Act 2016 and other applicable law), you can ask to access, correct, export (portability) or delete your data, object to or restrict certain processing, and withdraw consent. California residents can also ask what data we collect and how we use it, and will not be discriminated against for using these rights. We do not sell or “share” personal data as the CCPA defines those terms.

In the app, go to Account → Privacy & data to download your data (JSON export) or delete your account. You can also email devops@globocart.com. We reply within 30 days and may need to verify your identity first. In the EU or UK you can also complain to your data protection authority.

7. Deleting your account

See how to delete your GloboCart account, including the full list of what is deleted and what is kept. In short: sign in, go to Account → Privacy & data → “Delete my account now…”, and confirm with your password. If you signed up with Google or Apple and have no GloboCart password, in-app deletion is not available yet, so email us from your account’s address instead. Deletion is blocked until open orders, disputes, held escrow funds or unsettled seller payouts are resolved.

8. Children

GloboCart is not directed to children under 13, or the minimum age required in your country, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has given us data, contact us and we will delete it.

9. International transfers

GloboCart is an international marketplace. Your data may be processed in countries other than your own, including by our processors and carriers. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses or the UK equivalent.

10. Device permissions

The Android app requests only one permission: Internet access. It does not request location, notification, camera or photo/media permissions. Sellers who add listing photos pick them with the Android system file picker, which shares only the files you choose.

11. Changes

We will post any changes here and update the effective date. If a change is material, we will tell you in the app or by email.

12. Contact
