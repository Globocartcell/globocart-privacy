GloboCart Privacy Policy

Effective date: 14 October 2026 · Applies to the GloboCart Android app (com.globocart.app), the GloboCart web app (PWA) and globocart.com

This policy explains what personal data GloboCart (“we”, “us”) collects when you use the GloboCart marketplace, why we collect it, who we share it with, and the choices and rights you have. Contact: devops@globocart.com.

1. Data we collect

Data you give us

  • Account: email address, password (stored only as a one-way argon2 hash), display/profile details, language, time zone. If you choose Google or Apple sign-in, we receive your basic profile and email from that provider. If you enable them, we store multi-factor (TOTP) settings and passkey (WebAuthn) public credentials.
  • Addresses and contact: shipping and billing addresses and phone number. Sensitive address fields are encrypted at rest.
  • Orders and activity: orders, cart, wishlist, saved searches, reviews, buyer–seller messages, disputes and dispute evidence, and referrals.
  • Seller information (sellers only): legal or business name, country, registration number, tax ID, identity/business verification (KYC/KYB) status, storefront details, and listings with photos. Payout account details are held by our payment processor and are shown to us only in masked form.
  • Location (optional): your home country. If you tap “save location” in Account and allow it, your device’s precise GPS position. It is used for delivery estimates and local results, and is never collected in the background.
  • Photos you upload: listing, review or dispute images that you choose from your device.

Payment data

Card payments (Visa and Mastercard) are processed by our payment processor, Aegis, or by Stripe where the operator enables it. Your full card number and CVV are entered with and handled by the processor. GloboCart does not store them. We keep payment status, amount, currency, card brand and similar references needed for orders, refunds, escrow and disputes.

Data collected automatically

  • Security and fraud prevention: a hashed IP address, session and device/browser information, sign-in events, and order risk signals used by our fraud engine and sanctions screening.
  • Logs and audit trail: server logs and a tamper-evident audit log of security-relevant actions.
  • Usage within the service: product views, search queries and, for sellers, aggregate listing analytics. We do not use third-party advertising or analytics SDKs, and we do not sell your data or use it for cross-app tracking.
  • Push notifications (optional): if you allow notifications, a push subscription token is stored so we can send order, message and dispute updates.
  • Cookies and local storage: essential cookies (gc_session for sign-in, gc_csrf for security) and local storage for preferences such as language, currency and cart. There are no advertising cookies.

2. How we use data

  • To run your account and the marketplace: listings, checkout, payments, escrow (funds are held until delivery is confirmed), shipping, tracking, refunds and disputes.
  • For security, fraud and abuse prevention, sanctions screening, and content moderation.
  • For customer support and service messages (email and push).
  • Optional features: translating listings, reviews and messages, and the AI shopping assistant. The text you submit is sent to an AI provider to produce the result, and is used only for that purpose.
  • To meet legal duties such as tax, invoicing, accounting and anti-money-laundering.

Our legal bases (GDPR/UK GDPR) are: performance of the contract, legitimate interests (security, fraud prevention and service improvement), legal obligation, and your consent for optional location, push notifications and AI features. You can withdraw consent at any time.

3. Sharing

We share only what each party needs:

  • Sellers and buyers: when you order, the seller receives your name, shipping address and order details. Buyers see a seller’s public storefront.
  • Payment processors: Aegis, and Stripe where enabled, for payments, refunds and payouts.
  • Shipping carriers: DHL, FedEx, UPS or a regional courier receive the recipient name, address, phone and parcel details for rates, labels and tracking.
  • Service providers: hosting, database, file storage, email delivery, push services (browser and Google), and the AI translation/assistant provider. All of them act on our instructions.
  • Sign-in providers: Google and Apple, if you use them.
  • Authorities: where required by law, or to protect users and prevent fraud.

We do not sell personal data, and we do not share it for targeted advertising.

4. Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS). Passwords are hashed with argon2, and selected personal fields are encrypted at rest. We offer optional MFA and passkeys, use role-based staff access, rate limiting and a tamper-evident audit log. Data exports exclude credentials and bank details. No system is perfectly secure, and we do not claim any security certification we do not hold.

5. Retention

  • Account data is kept while your account is open.
  • Order, invoice, payment and KYC records are kept for as long as tax and anti-money-laundering law requires, typically 6 to 10 years. After an account is deleted these records are kept pseudonymised.
  • Security and audit logs are kept for as long as needed for security and legal purposes.
  • Sessions expire. Push subscriptions are removed when you turn notifications off or delete your account.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live (EU/EEA GDPR, UK GDPR, California CCPA/CPRA, Pakistan’s Prevention of Electronic Crimes Act 2016 and other applicable law), you can ask to access, correct, export (portability) or delete your data, object to or restrict certain processing, and withdraw consent. California residents can also ask what data we collect and how we use it, and will not be discriminated against for using these rights. We do not sell or “share” personal data as the CCPA defines those terms.

In the app, go to Account → Privacy & data to download your data (JSON export) or delete your account. You can also email devops@globocart.com. We reply within 30 days and may need to verify your identity first. In the EU or UK you can also complain to your data protection authority.

7. Deleting your account

See how to delete your GloboCart account. In short: sign in, then go to Account → Privacy & data → “Delete my account now…”, then confirm with your password. You can also email us from your account’s address. Deletion is blocked until open orders, disputes, held escrow funds or unsettled seller payouts are resolved. On deletion your sign-in, profile, addresses and contact details are removed or anonymised. Records the law requires us to keep are retained in pseudonymised form.

8. Children

GloboCart is not directed to children under 13, or the minimum age required in your country, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has given us data, contact us and we will delete it.

9. International transfers

GloboCart is an international marketplace. Your data may be processed in countries other than your own, including by our processors and carriers. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses or the UK equivalent.

10. Device permissions

The Android app requests Internet access. It asks for location (when you choose to save your location), notifications (when you enable them) and access to photos or the camera (only when you choose to upload an image), at the moment you use each feature. You can deny or revoke any of them in your device settings.

11. Changes

We will post any changes here and update the effective date. If a change is material, we will tell you in the app or by email.

12. Contact
