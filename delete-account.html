Delete your GloboCart account

App: GloboCart (com.globocart.app) · Developer: GloboCart · Updated 15 October 2026

In the app

  1. Open GloboCart and sign in.
  2. Go to Account → Privacy & data. You can download a copy of your data first if you want one.
  3. Tap Delete my account now… and confirm with your password.

Signed up with Google or Apple? If your account has no GloboCart password, in-app deletion is not available yet. Use the email option below.

Without the app

Email devops@globocart.com from the email address on your account, with the subject “Delete my GloboCart account”. We will verify the request and complete it within 30 days.

What is deleted or anonymised

  • Your email address (replaced with a pseudonym), password, two-step verification (MFA) settings and email-verification status. Your account is closed and can no longer sign in.
  • Saved address contents: name, street lines, city, region, postal code, phone number and tax ID.
  • All active sessions and sign-in/reset tokens, and your in-app notifications.
  • Messages you wrote in disputes (replaced with “message removed”).
  • Sellers: active listings are taken down, and the legal name, registration number, tax ID and payout account reference are removed.

What is kept

  • Required by law, in pseudonymised form: orders, invoices, payments, payouts and tax records, and KYC/anti-money-laundering records, typically for 6 to 10 years. The country on saved addresses is kept with these records.
  • Security records: server logs (which include IP addresses) and the security audit log, for as long as security and legal duties require.
  • Not yet removed by the deletion process: your cart, wishlist, saved searches, recently viewed products, search history, web push subscriptions, passkeys, linked Google/Apple sign-in records, home country and saved location, crypto wallet address, buyer–seller order messages, reviews you posted, and referral codes. These stay linked to the closed, pseudonymised account and are no longer tied to your email address.

Deletion can’t be completed while you have open orders, disputes, escrow funds still being held, or unsettled seller payouts. Resolve those first.
